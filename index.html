About the numbers

Axis caps are per calendar year per customer: Atlas 30,000 Group A / 1,20,000 Group B; Magnus for Burgundy 2,00,000 / 8,00,000; Magnus 1,00,000 / 4,00,000. Values here assume the full cap is still open.
Axis changed ratios again on 23 Sep 2026 (BA, Cathay, Finnair, Qatar, Vietnam and Accor now convert at the lower rate). Check the ratio on the bank's portal before every transfer; transfers can't be reversed.
Award prices are the lowest (saver / off-peak) one-way prices seen in 2026. Seats at that price are limited, taxes and fuel surcharges are extra, and airlines change charts without notice. Find the seat on the airline's site first, then transfer.
